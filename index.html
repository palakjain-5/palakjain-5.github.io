Emergency Medical ID

This address hosts MediQR public medical cards. Each card lives at /card/<card-id> and is opened automatically when someone scans a MediQR QR code with the MediQR app installed.

If you followed a QR link and see this page, the card id may be incomplete — ask the card owner to re-share their QR code.

Built with MediQR · card data is shared voluntarily and limited to emergency fields.
